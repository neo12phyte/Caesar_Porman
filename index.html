About Me

I teach International Baccalaureate students across two complementary areas: Business Management, where students analyze organizations and make evidence-based decisions, and Personal and Professional Skills, where students develop the reflective, interpersonal, and self-management skills needed beyond school.

My classroom practice is built around clarity. I make expectations visible, break complex tasks into manageable steps, and use case studies, structured discussion, reflection, and portfolio evidence so students can demonstrate both what they know and how they are growing.

Strong learning is not only about the final answer — it is also about the thinking, reflection, and evidence that lead there.
