About Me

Business Thinking, Personal Growth

I am an international educator specializing in IBDP Business Management, IGCSE Business Studies, and IB Career-related Programme Personal and Professional Skills (CP PPS). My teaching connects organizational decision-making, business strategy, assessment literacy, reflection, communication, and career-readiness skills.

My classroom practice combines real-world business cases, explicit assessment guidance, inquiry, structured feedback, digital portfolios, and educational technology. I make expectations visible, break complex tasks into manageable steps, and help students produce evidence of both academic understanding and personal growth.

Strong learning is not only about the final answer — it is also about the thinking, reflection, evidence, and decisions that lead there.
